# TTT-Closed-Universe

## Tri-Tetra Theory and the Closed-Universe Hypothesis

> **A research project exploring whether a closed, energy-filled universe can be formulated and tested without assuming a Big Bang origin.**

**Author:** Naoyuki Kawakami (川上真潔)  
**Project:** Tri-Tetra Theory (TTT)  
**Status:** Research / Open Hypothesis / Seeking Critical Collaboration

---

## 1. Overview

This repository develops a cosmological hypothesis emerging from the
**Tri-Tetra Theory (TTT)**.

The central question is:

> **Can the observed universe be described as a closed dynamical system
> filled with energy, without assuming that the universe began from a
> singular Big Bang state?**

TTT proposes that existence, motion, energy, mass, and geometry may be
different manifestations of a common dynamical structure.

The project therefore explores three interconnected ideas:

1. **Bipolar Zero** — a balanced state containing opposing potentials.
2. **Geometric Dynamics** — motion and energy generating stable geometric structures.
3. **Closed-Universe Dynamics** — the universe as a self-contained energetic and geometric system.

This repository does **not** claim that these hypotheses have been experimentally
confirmed.

Its purpose is to make them sufficiently explicit that they can be:

- mathematically examined,
- numerically simulated,
- compared with established physics,
- confronted with astronomical observations,
- and, most importantly, **falsified if they fail**.

The working hypothesis of this project is:

> **The universe may be a closed dynamical system whose fundamental description
> does not require an initial creation event from an absolute void.**

3. What TTT Is Trying to Reconsider

TTT does not begin by rejecting every result of modern physics.

Instead, it asks whether some familiar physical concepts can be understood
from a different underlying structure.

The principal questions are:

3.1 Cosmological origin

Is a Big Bang origin mathematically and observationally necessary?

Or can the known observations be reproduced by a closed dynamical universe?

3.2 Space

Is space a fundamental entity, or can spatial structure emerge from relations,
energy distributions, and motion?

3.3 Energy

Can the universe be treated as a closed energetic system rather than a system
whose fundamental description requires an initial singularity?

3.4 Mass

Can mass be interpreted geometrically as energy and motion becoming confined
or stabilized within a closed structure?

3.5 Gravity

Can phenomena normally described as gravity be reproduced from energy
distribution and geometric constraints without assuming gravity as an
independent fundamental interaction?

3.6 Expansion

Can the observed cosmic expansion be interpreted as a dynamical evolution
of a closed system rather than necessarily as the creation of new space
from an initial Big Bang?

These questions must ultimately be answered by mathematics and observation,
not philosophical preference.

5. Bipolar Zero

A central concept of TTT is the Bipolar Zero.

The symbol 0 does not necessarily represent absolute absence.

Instead, it represents a balanced state in which opposing potentials coexist.
